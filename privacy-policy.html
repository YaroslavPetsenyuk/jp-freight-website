Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

JP Freight Logistics LLC ("JP Freight," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, request a freight quote, apply as a carrier, contact us, or choose to receive SMS/MMS messages from us.

We do not sell or rent personal information. SMS/MMS opt-in data, consent records, and mobile numbers will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, company, email address, phone number, shipment origin and destination, equipment needs, pickup date, shipment details, motor carrier number, USDOT number, and other information submitted through our forms, by phone, by email, or through SMS/MMS.

When you use our website, we or our service providers may also collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, pages visited, and approximate location derived from an IP address.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries; prepare and manage freight quotes; coordinate shipments; evaluate and onboard carriers; provide customer service; communicate about requests, services, and account or operational matters; maintain safety, security, and legal compliance; prevent fraud or misuse; improve our website and services; and keep appropriate business records.

How We Share Information

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, form processing, communications, technology, and professional advisers. These providers may use information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, in connection with a business transaction, or at your direction.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. In all cases, SMS/MMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS/MMS Communications

If you voluntarily check an SMS consent box or otherwise provide express consent, JP Freight Logistics LLC may send text messages related to your quote request, carrier onboarding, shipment coordination, customer service, or other services you requested. Consent to receive texts is optional and is not a condition of purchasing or receiving services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you may receive one confirmation message. Reply HELP for help where supported, call (916) 252-6621, or email info@jpfreightlogistics.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We maintain SMS consent records as reasonably necessary to document your preferences and comply with applicable requirements. SMS/MMS opt-in data, consent, and mobile numbers are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies and Site Data

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for site operation, security, performance, or form functionality. Third-party services used on the site may process technical data under their own privacy terms. You can adjust cookie controls through your browser, although blocking some technologies may affect site functionality.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and consent records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. You may unsubscribe from SMS/MMS messages by replying STOP and may contact us regarding other communication preferences.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.
